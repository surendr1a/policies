Privacy Policy for Bhakti Ghar

Effective date: 20 July 2026

Bhakti Ghar ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Bhakti Ghar mobile application.

Information We Collect

The current version of Bhakti Ghar does not require registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your selected language, guest session, settings, and Sankalp progress may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and progress. This information is not transmitted to us and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties. The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Bhakti Ghar does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current version does not collect personal data, no child-specific personal information is retained by us.

Security

App preferences and progress are kept locally using the device's app storage. Although we use reasonable technical measures, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You can delete locally stored app information at any time by using your device settings to clear Bhakti Ghar's storage or by uninstalling the app. No online account is created in the current version.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version introduces accounts, analytics, advertising, payments, or server-side data processing, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those features are released.
