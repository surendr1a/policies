Privacy PolicyEffective Date: July 25, 2026 [2]

This Privacy Policy explains how our LinkedIn Automation App ("the App") collects, uses, 
and handles your data when you interact with our automated LinkedIn tools.

1. Data We CollectWe only access data that you explicitly authorize through the 
LinkedIn OAuth permissions screen, which may include:Your basic LinkedIn profile 
information (Name, Member URN).Content, posts, and comments interacting with our
specific published posts.

2. How We Use Your DataWe use the collected information strictly to fuel automated
interactions requested by the user, specifically:To programmatically publish 
automated PDF carousels and text updates to your authorized feed.To scan comments 
on our specific posts and automatically send the requested resource link via 
LinkedIn Direct Message (DM).

3. Data Storage and RetentionZero Data Retention: We do not host, store,
or sell your personal LinkedIn data or message histories on any external 
database.Data is processed instantly in temporary runtime memory 
(Cloudflare Workers / Serverless Scripts) and dropped immediately 
after the API action is completed.

4. Third-Party SharingWe do not sell, rent, or trade your personal
data with third parties. All data transmissions occur securely and
directly between our backend server script and official LinkedIn API endpoints.

5. Your Rights and RevocationYou can revoke the App's access to your
LinkedIn account at any absolute time by navigating to your personal
LinkedIn settings under "Permitted Services" and removing our App's permissions.

6. Contact UsFor any privacy-related queries regarding this automation pipeline,
please open an issue on our official GitHub repository hosting this file.
