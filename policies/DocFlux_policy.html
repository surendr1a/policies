Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocFlux ("DocFlux", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the DocFlux mobile application. DocFlux provides tools to convert, merge, compress, and unlock PDF and image files.

Information we access or collect

  • Files you select: DocFlux accesses only the PDF or image files that you choose through the system file picker, for the purpose of completing the operation you request.
  • Anonymous app identifier: the app creates a random identifier and stores it securely on your device. We use it to track free usage limits, associate processing jobs, prevent abuse, and connect subscription status. It is not your advertising ID and does not directly identify you.
  • Usage and processing information: operation type, usage count, processing status, timestamps, and technical error information needed to provide and maintain the service.
  • Purchase information: subscription status, product purchased, and transaction-related identifiers are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

DocFlux does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or advertising ID.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • perform the document operation you request and return the result;
  • manage daily usage limits and subscription access;
  • restore purchases, prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain security;
  • diagnose failures and improve the reliability of DocFlux; and
  • meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information or use your documents for advertising, profiling, or training artificial-intelligence models.

Document processing and retention

When an operation requires cloud processing, the files you explicitly select are transmitted using an encrypted connection to private storage and the processing service. Input and output files are intended to be automatically and permanently deleted within approximately three minutes after processing. Temporary copies may be removed earlier. You should download your result promptly. We do not manually inspect file contents except where strictly necessary to investigate a security incident or comply with law.

Anonymous usage records, job status records, and purchase-entitlement records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, prevent abuse, meet accounting or legal requirements, and resolve disputes. They are deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

Service providers and sharing

We disclose limited information only to service providers that help operate DocFlux:

  • Supabase provides backend database, private file storage, and related infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements and purchase status.
  • Google Play processes Android app distribution and in-app payments.
  • Cloud processing providers may temporarily receive selected files solely to perform the requested conversion or document operation.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Security and international transfers

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted transmission, private storage, restricted access, and short retention for document files. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Our providers may process information in countries outside your own; where required, appropriate legal safeguards are used for such transfers.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid file access by not selecting a file. You may clear locally stored app data by uninstalling DocFlux or using your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your information, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To make a privacy or deletion request, contact us using the address below. We may ask for information needed to verify and locate the relevant anonymous identifier.

Children's privacy

DocFlux is a general-audience productivity application and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in their country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to DocFlux, our providers, or applicable law. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app.
