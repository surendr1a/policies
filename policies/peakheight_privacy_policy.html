Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Last updated: October 15, 2026

PeakHeight ("PeakHeight," "we," "us," or "our") provides estimated adult-height projections, growth tracking, wellness guidance, habit routines, reminders, and related features through the PeakHeight mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used and disclosed, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide:

  • an optional name;
  • date of birth and sex selection;
  • current height and weight;
  • optional parent and sibling heights;
  • region and growth-related information, such as puberty stage and recent growth-spurt status;
  • wellness information, such as sleep duration, activity level, diet quality, and posture self-assessment;
  • a goal height, unit preference, and notification preference;
  • later height and weight check-ins; and
  • habit and routine completion information.

Some of this information may be considered health, wellness, personal, or sensitive information under applicable law. Please provide only information that you are comfortable sharing.

B. Anonymous account and device information

PeakHeight does not require an email address or traditional account registration. The App creates a random device identifier and a random user identifier to create and maintain an anonymous account. We may also process your device platform, App version, language or locale, time-zone offset, authentication token, and timestamps such as account creation and last activity.

The random device identifier is generated by the App. It is not your Android advertising ID, phone number, IMEI, or hardware serial number.

C. App activity and generated information

We process information about your use of the App, including onboarding progress and responses, prediction results, check-in history, habit completion, streaks, reminder settings, language selection, ad impressions and clicks, and interactions with reports and routines.

PeakHeight generates estimates such as predicted adult height, confidence ranges, percentiles, growth-window estimates, and trend information from the information you provide. These results are estimates and are not medical diagnoses.

D. Advertising, analytics, and diagnostics

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising. Subject to your consent choices, age-appropriate settings, and applicable law, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • device and app identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers where permitted;
  • consent signals and ad-personalization choices;
  • ad impressions, clicks, and other ad interactions;
  • device, browser, operating-system, and App information; and
  • diagnostics and fraud-prevention information.

The App uses Google consent tools where required. For users identified as children or under the age of consent, the App sends age-restriction signals and requests child-directed or non-personalized, age-appropriate advertising as applicable. PeakHeight does not send height, weight, birthdate, predictions, check-ins, or habit history to AdMob for ad targeting.

The App also uses Firebase Analytics to understand App usage and Firebase Crashlytics for crash and diagnostic reporting. Firebase may process a random App user ID, app-instance identifiers, device and App information, usage events, onboarding events and responses, ad interaction events, crash traces, and related diagnostics. Google processes this information under its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

For information about how Google processes data, visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

E. Permissions and information stored on your device

The App requests internet access to communicate with our backend and advertising or analytics services. It may request notification permission so that it can schedule wellness and check-in reminders. Reminder schedules and some App preferences or cached reports are stored locally on your device. You can deny or revoke notification permission through your device settings.

The App does not request precise or approximate device-location permission, camera access, microphone access, contacts, or phone-number access.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and maintain your anonymous App session;
  • calculate and display height projections, ranges, percentiles, and trends;
  • personalize routines, habits, reminders, tips, and reports;
  • save and synchronize onboarding responses, measurements, progress, and preferences;
  • provide customer support and respond to privacy requests;
  • operate, maintain, debug, secure, and improve the App and backend;
  • measure feature usage, App performance, and advertising performance;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, automated attacks, and security incidents;
  • display ads and manage consent or privacy choices; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Where applicable, our legal bases may include performing the service you request, your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the App, and compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is required for personalized advertising or similar processing, you may withdraw or change that consent through the available privacy controls.

3. How We Disclose Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may disclose or allow processing of information in the following circumstances:

  • Cloud hosting: Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts our backend, API, logs, and databases.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: Google Firebase helps us measure App usage and diagnose crashes.
  • Advertising and consent: Google AdMob and participating advertising partners deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and record consent choices.
  • Legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or government requests; protect users, the public, or our rights; or investigate fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Business changes: Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of the service, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not provide body measurements, growth predictions, check-ins, or wellness responses to AdMob for interest-based ad targeting. Depending on your location, the use of advertising identifiers or personalized advertising may legally be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising." Where required, the App provides consent or opt-out controls.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Backend information is stored using AWS services in the AWS region configured for the service. Information is generally kept while your anonymous account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain security, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations.

Operational backend logs are generally configured for a 14-day retention period. Some temporary security or webhook-deduplication records may be retained for up to 30 days. Google, Firebase, AdMob, and their partners retain information according to their own settings, terms, and legal requirements.

Information stored locally on your device remains until it is deleted through the App, cleared through device settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled, subject to your device's behavior.

When you use the in-App deletion feature, we delete the anonymous user record and associated onboarding answers, predictions, check-ins, and habit-completion records from the active backend databases. Limited records may remain temporarily where required for security, legal compliance, backup integrity, or fraud prevention, and third-party providers may retain information under their own policies.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Data sent between the App and our backend is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to production systems is restricted, and backend permissions are limited by role. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, or receive a portable copy of certain information.

You can:

  • update onboarding information by using the redo-onboarding feature where available;
  • manage notification permission in your device settings;
  • review or change advertising consent choices through Profile → Ad privacy choices when the option is required or available;
  • delete your anonymous account and associated backend data through Profile → Delete my data; and
  • contact us using the email address below for a privacy request.

Because the App uses an anonymous account, we may need information such as your App user ID to locate a record. We may ask for reasonable verification before completing a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

PeakHeight is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 18 should use the App with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian. The App is not intended for children under 13, and children under 13 should not submit personal information through the App.

If we learn that a child under the applicable minimum age has provided personal information without required parental authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian may contact us at the email address below to request deletion.

Where the App has age information indicating that a user is a child or is under the applicable age of consent, advertising requests are configured with age-appropriate restrictions. This does not replace any parental-consent requirement that may apply under local law.
