Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026

Privacy at a glance: The current version of VaultMail does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or analytics.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VaultMail ("VaultMail", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the VaultMail Android application (the "App"). It applies to the App identified by the package name io.vaultmail.app.

2. Information we collect

The current version of the App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information, device identifiers, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, advertising data, analytics data, or diagnostic data.

Temporary inbox addresses and the messages shown in the current App are generated and processed locally on your device. We do not receive or store them on our servers.

3. Device permissions and local processing

The App declares Internet access so that online features can be supported. The current version does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, call logs, or device storage.

Information displayed by the App may remain temporarily in the App's memory while it is running. The App is configured to prevent Android cloud backup of its application data.

4. How we use and share information

Because the current version does not collect personal information, we do not use or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. We do not sell personal information.

5. Purchases

If paid features are made available through Google Play, payment processing will be handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We will not receive your full payment-card details. This policy will be updated before we introduce any purchase-related data collection beyond information provided by Google Play to operate and verify a purchase.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of App users in the current version, so we do not retain personal information from the App. You can clear locally held App data through Android settings or remove it by uninstalling the App.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect the App and any information it processes. However, no device, software, or method of electronic transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

VaultMail is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. International users

VaultMail is available to users in multiple countries. As the current version does not transmit personal information to us, we do not transfer App user data to our servers in another country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will provide appropriate notice where required.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email us at privacy@vaultmail.io.
