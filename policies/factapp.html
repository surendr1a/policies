PRIVACY POLICY

true. Daily Facts

Effective September 26, 2026

true. Daily Facts is designed to work without an account and to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what stays on your device, when network services are contacted, and the choices available to you.

Information stored on your device

The app stores saved facts, viewed-fact history, a randomly generated feed-order preference, and your reminder settings locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app features you select. It is not uploaded to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes this local information.

Information we do not collect

We do not require an account and do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, payment details, photos, files, or analytics data. The current release contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Network services and third parties

The app can display externally hosted images and may retrieve fact content from our AWS-hosted API in future releases. Image and source providers, web browsers, internet service providers, AWS, and Google Play may process standard technical information needed to deliver their services, such as IP address, device/browser details, request time, and the requested URL. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

Content sources and image credits

Fact descriptions are derived from Wikidata under CC0. Images come from source-linked media repositories and retain their individual licences and attribution metadata. Use the information icon on an image, or its credit in Saved Facts, to open the original media page and licence details.

Notifications

If you opt in, Android schedules your selected reminders locally. The app requests notification permission only when you enable this feature. We do not collect a push token or upload your reminder preference. You can disable reminders in the app or Android settings at any time.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app has no account and does not upload your saved facts or preferences, we do not retain an account profile to delete. You can remove saved facts individually, clear all app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Operational infrastructure records, if generated in a future API-enabled release, are retained only as needed for security, reliability, and legal obligations.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. We apply reasonable technical safeguards to our infrastructure, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience educational product and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated. For privacy questions, use the developer contact mechanism displayed on the true. Daily Facts Google Play listing.
