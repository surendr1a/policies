Privacy Policy

Factapp is designed to help users read and explore informational fact cards. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to operate and improve the app.

Information We May Collect

  • Account information, such as name, email address, or login details, if the app offers registration or authentication.
  • App activity information, such as saved cards, bookmarks, comments, preferences, and similar in-app actions.
  • Device and usage information, such as device type, app version, crash logs, diagnostics, and basic analytics.
  • Content submitted by users, such as comments or feedback, when such features are available.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Factapp.
  • To save user preferences, bookmarks, comments, and app state.
  • To detect bugs, prevent abuse, and keep the app secure.
  • To respond to support requests or policy-related questions.

We do not sell personal information. These providers are expected to use the information only for the services they provide to Factapp.
